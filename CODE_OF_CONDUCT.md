# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want AgentGuard to be a welcoming, respectful, and technically rigorous
project for everyone who participates in its development or community.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence rather than people;
- welcome different backgrounds and levels of experience;
- document technical decisions clearly;
- respect the project’s scope, security boundaries, and review process; and
- take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- publishing private or identifying information without permission;
- threats, deliberate disruption, or malicious repository changes;
- impersonation, doxxing, or targeted abuse; and
- knowingly bypassing security controls or encouraging others to do so.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces, issue and pull-request discussions,
reviews, events, and other interactions when someone is representing the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the project host’s private reporting channel
when that channel is enabled. If no private channel is available, open a
minimal public issue requesting a private channel and avoid publishing
sensitive details.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and may take proportionate action,
including a warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent
removal from project spaces. Reports are handled on a need-to-know basis where
practical. No response time or outcome is guaranteed.

Retaliation against a person who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.
False reports made deliberately to harass someone are also a violation of this
policy.

## Attribution

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant community standards and
tailored to AgentGuard’s repository and security-boundary requirements.
